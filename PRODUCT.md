# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Restaurant owners & managers** (admin): oversee menu items, manage multiple branches, track orders, configure storefront content (hero banners, featured dishes), monitor operations.

**Customers** (public): discover the restaurant's offerings, browse menu by category, customize orders, make reservations, check locations, place online orders with personal touch.

Both audiences matter equally; the system serves both seamlessly.

## Product Purpose

RestoCMS is a complete digital storefront and management platform for artisanal food businesses (pastry shops, restaurants, catering). It enables owners to showcase their craft, manage inventory and orders, and engage customers directly—while giving customers a premium, personal way to discover, customize, and purchase. Success means owners spend less time on logistics and more on craft; customers feel the warmth and care behind every order.

## Brand Personality

Warm, inviting, personal. The brand is craft-focused and heritage-rich—artisanal, not industrial. It values the human touch: hand-made quality, personal attention, relationships over transactions. Voice is conversational and genuine, never corporate or slick.

## Anti-references

- Cold, corporate SaaS dashboards (Shopify admin tone)
- Overly technical or feature-heavy interfaces (no jargon for customers)
- Generic e-commerce UX (Wix / Squarespace templates)
- Impersonal or transactional design language
- Trendy, algorithm-driven aesthetics (TikTok-style feeds, infinite scroll)

## Design Principles

1. **Craft before commerce** — Every interface choice reflects artisanal care. Premium materials (generous spacing, considered typography, restrained color), not discount aesthetics.
2. **Personal connection first** — Features that build relationships (staff introductions, story-telling about ingredients, personal order notes, WhatsApp integration) rank above convenience shortcuts.
3. **Clarity without clutter** — Information architecture serves the job (finding dishes, placing orders, managing stock), never the system. No unnecessary steps, layers, or marketing noise.
4. **Heritage visible** — Design pays respect to tradition and craftsmanship. Typography, color (burgundy, gold, cream), and imagery feel timeless, not trendy.
5. **Trust through restraint** — Premium means less is more. Fewer colors, fewer typefaces, fewer animations. Every detail earned its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Focus on:
- Sufficient color contrast (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text)
- Keyboard navigation throughout (especially admin interfaces)
- Screen reader support for order flow and menu structure
- Touch targets ≥48px for mobile interactions (ordering, navigation)
- Reduced motion support for all animations
